# App Store Connect privacy configuration

Use this checklist when completing issue #31 in App Store Connect.

- Privacy Policy URL: `https://mdunavant07.github.io/gametracker-privacy/privacy/`
- Support contact: `gametracker@geneticlyperfct.com`
- The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
- The app does not track people across apps or websites.
- Game records, collection names, birthdays, and photos are entered by the person using the app and are stored locally and through their iCloud account for app functionality.
- Collection content can be shared only when the owner initiates a CloudKit share. Invited participants receive the access selected by the owner.
- Venue-derived city strings are sent to Apple's geocoding service for app functionality. The app does not request the device's current location.
- Backups are created only at the person's request and are sent only to destinations they choose through the system share sheet.

Review these answers against the current App Store Connect privacy questionnaire immediately before submission because Apple may change its questionnaire and definitions.
