District Game Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 25, 2026

District Game Tracker helps you keep a personal record of sporting events you have attended. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

District Game Tracker handles information that you choose to enter or add:

  • Game records, including dates, teams, opponents, scores, leagues, game types, venues, and optional notes or league details.
  • A collection name and optional birthday used to calculate statistics.
  • Photos you select from your photo library or take with the camera. The app does not scan your photo library; it receives only the photos you select through Apple's system photo picker.

How information is used

This information is used only to provide app features such as displaying and editing your collection, calculating statistics, showing a photo gallery, creating backups, and synchronizing or sharing a collection through iCloud. District Game Tracker does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and the developer does not sell your data or use it to track you across apps or websites.

Storage and iCloud synchronization

Your collection is stored in the app's local database on your device. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit synchronizes the collection, birthday, game records, and photos through the iCloud account signed in on your devices. Apple processes and stores that information as the iCloud service provider under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

The developer does not operate a separate District Game Tracker account system or server and does not routinely receive your collection. The developer cannot access the private CloudKit data associated with your personal iCloud account through the app. If you deliberately send a backup, screenshot, or other information when requesting support, the developer can access the material you send.

CloudKit sharing

If you choose to share a collection, Apple creates a CloudKit share. People you invite can access the collection name, birthday, game records, and photos in that shared collection. Depending on the permission you assign, a participant may have View or View & Edit access. Participants with edit permission can change or delete shared content, and their changes synchronize to other participants. Do not share a collection with anyone you do not trust.

City geocoding and maps

District Game Tracker derives city names from venue text in your game records to create the cities-visited map. Those city strings are submitted to Apple's geocoding service to obtain map coordinates. District Game Tracker does not request or collect your device's current location. Map previews and resolved coordinates may be cached on your device to improve performance.

Backups and exports

When you create a backup, District Game Tracker writes a file containing the collection name, optional birthday, and game records. If you select “Include Photos,” the backup also contains your saved photos. You decide where to send or save an exported backup using Apple's share sheet. Exported files are outside District Game Tracker's control; deleting the app or its data does not delete copies you saved elsewhere or gave to another person.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device and in iCloud until you delete it, remove the app's iCloud data, or otherwise manage it through Apple’s iCloud settings. Deleting a game or photo in the app removes it from the active collection and synchronizes that change through CloudKit. Owners can remove eligible collections and manage CloudKit sharing. Apple may retain backups or service records for a limited period under its own policies.

You are responsible for deleting exported backup files from every location where you stored or shared them. A share participant may also retain information they previously viewed or exported.

Security

District Game Tracker relies on iOS data protection and Apple's iCloud and CloudKit security. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so use care when sharing collections or exported backups.

Children's privacy

District Game Tracker is not designed to collect information directly from children and does not provide public profiles, advertising, or a developer-operated account system. A birthday entered for statistics is stored as part of the collection as described above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when District Game Tracker's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

Contact and deletion assistance

For privacy questions or help locating and deleting District Game Tracker data, email gametracker@geneticlyperfct.com. Because the developer does not control your private iCloud account, the developer may provide instructions but may be unable to delete private CloudKit data on your behalf.
